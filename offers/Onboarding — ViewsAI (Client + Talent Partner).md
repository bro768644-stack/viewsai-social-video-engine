## 🔄 Step 3 — How we work

**Weekly rhythm**
- **Monday:** priorities for the week, agreed in writing

- Everything of record lives in the inbox (so nothing depends on someone's memory)

**What we never do**

**Weekly client report** — what we did, what we found, what's blocked, what's next.

## 📞 Step 5 — Candidates and interviews

**Interview scheduling is done for you.** We coordinate both sides, confirm, and remind.

You own the data. If we part ways, it stays with you.

# PART 2 — TALENT PARTNER ONBOARDING
### *You just joined the delivery team.*

## 👋 Welcome to the team

You're joining ViewsAI as a talent partner. You have AI infrastructure behind you that most
recruiters never get: sourcing agents, an outbound engine, a behaviour graph, and a content
machine. Your job is judgement — who to call, what to ask, what to close.

**Bookmark this page.** It replaces every "how do I…" message.

## 💼 LinkedIn profile setup

**Your profile is a ViewsAI asset.** Set it up once, properly:

- Headline: `[Specialism] Recruiter · ViewsAI` — specificity beats cleverness
- About: two short paragraphs. What roles you fill, and who you fill them for. No buzzwords.
- Featured: one case study or candidate story
- Activity: post or comment weekly. We supply the content.
- Banner: ViewsAI branded (we provide the file)

**Rule:** you never misrepresent your employer or your access to candidates. Ever.

## 🤑 Submitting a new job order

A job order is only real when it has all five:

1. **Role and must-haves** at the top of the resume, not buried in it
2. **Compensation range** (we do not run searches without one)
3. **Location / work arrangement**
4. **Why it's hard** (the honest reason)
5. **Hiring manager and decision process** (who says yes, and after what)

**Then it goes into the pipeline** and the sourcing agent starts immediately. You do not wait for
a weekly kickoff meeting. Speed is the product.

## 🦸 Sourcing — how to find candidates

We don't apply-to-job-board posting. We source **passive candidates**:

- The source agent turns your search into a target list of people who match the must-haves
- Every candidate gets verified contact data before anyone reaches out
- Outbound sequences run on warmed mailboxes with rotating senders
- Replies route back to your inbox and to the behaviour graph
- You see who opened, who clicked, who came back to the site twice — then you call the hot ones

**Your job:** work the signal, not the list. The graph tells you who is actually warm.

## ⛽ Candidate Present package (AI prompt)

Before you present a candidate, use the generator. Prompts are in the ViewsAI prompt library.

**A present package includes:**
- Candidate summary — the 3-line version a hiring manager actually reads
- Match rationale — mapped line-by-line to the must-haves
- What they need — the honest gaps and what would make them move
- Availability and compensation reality

**Rule:** never present a candidate you haven't spoken to. The AI drafts, you verify.

## 🤑 Marketing your candidates

A great candidate we don't market is a candidate we don't place.

- Post the candidate's anonymised story to your network
- Send them to 3+ matching hiring managers, not one
- Follow up on a schedule you can actually keep
- Log every touch so the graph scores them correctly

## 💸 Closing report

Written the day a placement is confirmed. It is the document that triggers billing, so it matters.

**Contents:** role, candidate, agreed compensation, start date, fee basis, signed terms attached,
and what we learned. **Nothing bills without it.**

## 📝 Weekly client reports

Every Friday, for every active client, in writing:

- What we did this week
- Candidates in play and their stage
- What's blocked and what you need from the client
- What's next week

**Send it even when the week was bad.** Especially then.

## 📝 Weekly accomplishment sheet

Your own record, submitted weekly: roles worked, candidates sourced, presents sent, interviews
booked, placements closed. This is how you and the system see your own output over time.

## 📞 Candidate call framework

Every candidate call covers, in this order:

1. **Role** — what they're doing now, in their words
2. **Motivation** — the actual reason they'd move (not "career growth")
3. **Must-haves** — verify each one against the job order
4. **Compensation** — base, bonus, what they need vs what they'll accept
5. **Timing** — notice period, competing processes, decision timeline
6. **The close** — "if this were the right role, what would need to be true?"

**Then you write it up and present it.** No present without a call.

## 💽 Your systems (the ATS)

Forget juggling tabs. Everything lands in one place:

| System | What it does |
|---|---|
| **ViewsAI inbox** (Chatwoot) | Every reply, DM and channel, with the drafted response waiting for your approval |
| **Pipeline** (Supabase + CRM) | Every candidate and client, with full activity history |
| **Behaviour graph** | Opens, clicks, page views, downloads, replies — scored and tiered |
| **Outbound engine** | Warm-up rotation, verifier gate, send queue, reply capture |
| **Content engine** | One master video or post → every platform, with text overlay and voice |

You don't need to memorise the tools. You need to know that **the graph tells you who is hot** and
**the inbox tells you who is waiting.**

## 🧞 Job description generator

Paste the hiring manager's notes, get a description that senior candidates actually answer.
Prompts are in the ViewsAI prompt library. **Edit the output before it goes anywhere near a job board.**

## 💻 Email prompt

The outbound copy is drafted for you and humanised before it sends: contractions, varied rhythm,
no em dashes, no template tells. Under 120 words, one clear ask.

**Your job:** approve, adjust the personal line, and let the sequence run.
