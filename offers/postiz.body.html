Do I need a server?

A small VPS is ideal (we'll recommend one, usually $5–12/month). If you'd rather not run a server, we can host it for you — ask us.

Is this legal / allowed by the platforms?

Postiz is open-source software using each platform's official APIs and OAuth — the same mechanism the paid schedulers use.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing. It's your server and your install. There's no subscription to cancel and nothing to cut off.

Can my team use it?

Yes — unlimited team members, no per-seat pricing. That's the whole point.

Do you support API-only channels?

X, LinkedIn, YouTube, Meta and more connect through official apps. We'll tell you upfront if a specific account needs extra setup.
