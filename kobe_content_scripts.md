# Kobe Content Engine — Scripts & Format (2026-09-02)

**Format:** Kobe (or Brendan) says the hook (real dialogue, quoted in Veo prompt) →
text overlay pivots to value → CTA routes to lead magnet.
*"Kobe gets the attention; ViewsAI gets the credibility; the business outcome gets the conversion."*

**Pillars:** more pipeline → faster hiring → automated GTM.
Avoid over-selling "AI" — sell "the system that gets the business result."

## 🔥 Outbound / Lead Gen (pillar: more pipeline)
1. "Your sales team doesn't need more leads. They need better outbound."
2. "Still paying humans to find your prospects? That's adorable."
3. "Your competitors are automating outbound. Are you?"
4. "What if your outbound team worked 24/7?"
5. "You don't need 10 SDRs. You need a better system."
6. "Your CRM is full. Your pipeline isn't."
7. "Cold email isn't dead. Bad cold email is."
8. "Stop buying leads. Build your own pipeline."
9. "Your next customer might already be in your database."
10. "If your outbound depends on humans doing spreadsheets..."

## 👔 Recruiting (pillar: faster hiring)
1. "You don't need another recruiter. You need recruiting automation."
2. "Still sourcing candidates one LinkedIn profile at a time?"
3. "Your recruiter isn't slow. Your process is."
4. "What if your recruiting team never stopped sourcing?"
5. "You're paying recruiters to search. AI can do that."
6. "Your best candidate probably isn't applying."
7. "Job boards show you applicants. We find the people you actually want."
8. "Your open role isn't the problem. Finding the right people is."
9. "Imagine having a recruiter working every hour you're not."
10. "Stop waiting for candidates to apply."

## 🤖 ViewsAI / AI Agents (pillar: automated GTM)
1. "What if your company could hire its own AI workforce?"
2. "You're not replacing your team. You're giving them AI coworkers."
3. "This is what an AI employee actually looks like."
4. "Your business has repetitive work. AI should have it."
5. "Why hire another employee for work AI can handle?"
6. "Imagine your business running while you sleep."
7. "We build AI agents that actually do the work."
8. "Your next hire might not need a salary."
9. "AI isn't coming for your job. It's coming for your workload."
10. "This little dog knows more about AI than your sales team."
